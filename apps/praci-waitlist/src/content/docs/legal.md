The Legal page lists your contracts and the dates that matter, taken from the files in your [Data room](/docs/data-room).

![The Praci Legal page with a contract table](/docs/legal.webp)

*Demo data.*

## What you see

- Counts of contracts, active contracts, open deadlines and overdue deadlines.
- **Contracts.** Each contract with its parties, status, term, renewal terms (for example "Auto-renews, 62 days notice"), value and next deadline.
- **Deadlines.** The dates found in your contracts, with overdue ones marked.
- **Graph.** The relationships between your contracts and documents. Select an item to see how it connects and open the document.

## How it fills in

The Legal agent reads your Data room and extracts contracts and deadlines. Press **Scan now** to run it. Once you allow access, it also scans daily.

The first time, the page asks you to **Allow** the Legal agent to read your documents. The prompt names the service that receives the text. It also says which model Legal uses: the Legal agent currently runs on one specific model, Muse Spark 1.2, through your own OpenRouter key. If the key is missing or the model was changed, the page tells you what to fix.

**Ask** opens the Legal agent, so you can ask "Who still owes us a signed NDA?"

Praci reads and organizes contracts. It does not give legal advice.
