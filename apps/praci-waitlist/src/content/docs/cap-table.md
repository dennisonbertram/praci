The Cap table page shows who owns your company. It reads the data from **Carta**. Praci does not keep its own cap table, so to change ownership you record the change in Carta.

![The Praci Cap table page](/docs/cap-table.webp)

*Demo data.*

## Connect Carta

Connect Carta from the Cap table page or from [Connect apps](/docs/integrations). You sign in on Carta's own page. The **Carta agent** then reads your cap table. Praci uses Carta in read-only mode.

## What you see

- Fully diluted shares, option pool available, the common share price from your latest 409A, and the amount raised.
- An ownership chart and a table of everyone on the cap table, which you can search, sort and filter by security.
- Tabs for Shareholders, Investors, Options, Auditors and Other.

After you record a new SAFE or priced round in Carta, press the refresh button. The Carta agent reads your cap table again and the page updates.

## Contacts

The tabs also hold your **contacts**: investors, auditors and counsel, with name, email, organization and notes. Contacts are your address book inside Praci. They do not change Carta. If you are not on Carta, you can still keep your investors and advisors here.

## Ask the Carta agent

**Ask** opens the Carta agent. Suggested prompts include "Who are our investors?" and "Draft this month's investor update."

- **Investor updates.** The agent uses your cash, burn and runway from [Finance](/docs/finance) and your ownership data from Carta to draft an update as a Gmail draft. You review it before anything is sent.
- **Auditor requests.** The agent can compare an auditor's request against Carta figures and draft a reply. Sending always needs your approval.

## Files

**Import** and **Add files** put documents into the Cap table folder of your [Data room](/docs/data-room), such as a 409A report or a signed SAFE.
