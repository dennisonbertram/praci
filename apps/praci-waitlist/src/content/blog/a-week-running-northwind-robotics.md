Northwind Robotics is a made-up company: ten people, picking robots for warehouses, one founder (Maya Chen) who also does finance, legal and hiring. Everything below uses the demo data that ships with Praci. The names, numbers and mail are invented. Each step names the part of Praci it uses, and links to its docs page.

![The Praci Inbox for Northwind Robotics](/docs/inbox.webp)

## Monday: triage investor email

Maya opens the [Inbox](/docs/inbox). Mail from both of her Gmail accounts is in one list. The first time, Praci asks whether it may sort her mail, and says which AI service will see the senders, subjects and previews. She clicks **Allow**.

Now the mail that **Needs reply** is at the top, and the **To do** list holds the follow-ups. A note from an investor asks for a Q3 update draft. Maya opens **Ask** and tells the Inbox agent to find the last update she sent and the thread with the investor. The agent searches her whole mail history, not only recent mail, and answers with the messages it found.

Nothing is sent. When the Inbox agent drafts a reply, Maya sees the full message and chooses **Send it** or **Edit first**.

## Tuesday: check runway before the board meeting

The board meets on the 28th. Maya opens [Finance](/docs/finance). It reads Northwind's Mercury and Brex accounts, read-only, and shows cash of $1,633,221, monthly burn of $67,621 and about 24 months of runway. That is demo data.

She clicks **Ask** and asks the Finance agent what runway looks like if the company hires two engineers. The agent uses the runway scenarios skill and the synced numbers, and gives her a figure she can put on a slide. She also looks at the **Upcoming 30 days** list, where a $2,000 legal bill is due on the 30th.

![Finance page for Northwind Robotics](/docs/finance.webp)

## Wednesday: prepare a data room for the raise

Investors will ask for contracts, the pitch deck and board minutes. Maya opens the [Data room](/docs/data-room) and drops in a folder of files. She imports a few more from Google Drive.

Praci reads each file, tags its type and parties, and files it. A PDF that cannot be read shows **Failed** with **Try again**, so she knows exactly which one needs a better copy. **Auto-sort** proposes a tidier folder layout and lists each move, and nothing changes until she presses **Apply**. Then she searches "pilot agreement" and finds both customer agreements by name and by text.

On [Legal](/docs/legal), she presses **Scan now**. The Legal agent lists four contracts, their renewal terms and one overdue deadline, which she had missed.

## Thursday: update the cap table after a SAFE

Northwind has signed a $250,000 SAFE with a fund. Maya's lawyer records it in Carta. On the [Cap table](/docs/cap-table) page she presses refresh, and the Carta agent reads Carta again. The page picks up the change from Carta. Praci does not keep its own cap table, so the change has to be in Carta first.

She adds the signed SAFE to the Data room's Cap table folder, then asks the Carta agent to draft the monthly investor update. The agent combines runway from Finance with ownership from Carta, and asks to save a Gmail draft addressed to the investors. Maya approves the draft and reads it before anything is sent.

![The Cap table page for Northwind Robotics](/docs/cap-table.webp)

## Friday: ask Chief for a hiring plan

Maya asks [Chief](/docs/bots-and-agents), "What should I hire for first?" Chief looks at cash and runway, and writes a hiring plan with two roles for Q4 and what they cost. The plan is saved as a document in [Artifacts](/docs/artifacts), where Maya can open it, and where Chief will add a new version if she asks for changes.

## Approving an agent's action

Through the week, bots kept asking before they acted. The investor update draft needed her approval to send. When a mail agent wanted to reply to a customer, Maya saw the message first. For an action she trusts, such as saving a Drive file, she can choose **Always allow this tool**. For anything she does not like, she chooses **Deny**, and nothing happens.

The full list of what asks, and the settings that tighten it, is on [Bots and agents](/docs/bots-and-agents#approvals) and [Privacy and safety](/docs/privacy-and-safety).
