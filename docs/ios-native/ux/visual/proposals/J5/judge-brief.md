Product: Praci, a planned native SwiftUI iPhone app (design boards only, no code). Problem: most pages describe inner (1/3 + 2/3), half-folded (1/2 + 1/2) and laptop poses only in rule-card text. Only Split (inner Inbox), Inner (half-folded Inbox + Inbox agent) and Desk (laptop Finance thread) are drawn. Each new board costs a 626x890 or 890x626 layout and must be kept in sync with other clusters that still edit Finance data, Inbox actions, Home and Computer copy. Decide which candidates get real boards now: Finance and Legal inner, Data room laptop, Inbox message inner, Computer laptop with keyboard.

Persona: a founder with a Duo who opens an email on the inner display and wants to archive or reply, and who takes control of the agent's computer in laptop pose and types.

Acceptance criteria: (1) the two poses with real interaction are drawn: open message with Archive, Reply, More (Cleanup) and Ask Inbox agent on the inner display; Computer in laptop pose with the system keyboard open, key bar and Release in the same slot as Take control; (2) the Desk rule card and the Desk board agree; (3) every page not drawn states which template it follows; (4) no board duplicates a decision already made elsewhere.

B (restructure): five new boards: Finance inner, Legal inner, Inbox message half-folded, Data room laptop, Computer laptop with keyboard; plus rule fixes.

Score each of A, B, C 1-5 on the six criteria in your system prompt, pick a winner and at most one graft.
