# Judge brief J5: Finance period control

Product: Praci, a planned native iPhone app. Persona: a startup founder opening Finance on a phone or the Duo second screen to see runway, cash and burn quickly. Finance has Runway ([N] mo), Cash, a 12-month Monthly burn bar chart, Accounts, Upcoming, a Hide amounts eye, and a Finance agent chat.

Problem: a 30 days / 90 days / 12 months control only lights bars; Runway, Cash and the chart never change. Also undecided: what the hidden-amounts frame masks. Rules: minimal copy (every word is UI), glass only on floating controls, solid content, native SwiftUI components, boards are static mockups with invented placeholders.

Options:
A: remove the control; label tiles "Runway now", "Cash today", chart "Monthly burn, 12 months"; lighter unselected bars plus baseline; hidden frame masks Runway and flattens bar heights; eye moves to top of the top half on Desk.
B: keep ranges as a menu pill in the burn card header; three data sets drawn (daily, weekly, monthly bars); tiles labeled as now.
C: Apple Stocks pattern: segmented range control under the chart, headline average follows range; three data sets drawn.

Score each 1-5 on: meets acceptance, simplicity, consistency, usability, visual quality, effort/risk. Return JSON with scores per option, winner, optional graft, short defense.
