# J4: Pending approval vs working indicator, and the one tinted action

## Problem
A finished draft still shows "Inbox agent is working"; the composer Send and the card Send are both prominent; a scrolled-away approval has no cue; Stop and Send share one slot with no rule for typed text. Findings: V-thread-R1-2, V-thread-R1-9, V-thread-R1-18, F-thread-014, F-thread-015, STORY-047-R1-2, STORY-047-R1-5, V-iphone-R1-13, F-duo-006, RED-R1-16.
Evidence: PG-A-Thread, PH-Thread (working line under the card, idle mic), PG-A-GroupThread (blue text for the Legal agent, whose avatar is purple), DS-06-Chat (composer spec: Send or Stop in one prominent slot; working = avatar, ring, dots, no text).

Acceptance criteria used: (1) no working line while a card awaits approval; (2) exactly one tinted action; (3) a tappable cue to a scrolled-away approval; (4) Stop, typing-while-running and Stopping composer states defined; (5) working indicator uses the shipped avatar colour and one spec on every board.

## Options considered
Weighted score out of 60 (criteria x3, simplicity, consistency, usability, visual x2, effort x1). Usability / Visual / Engineering lenses are my own scoring; the two external judges scored with the same rubric.

| Option | Idea | Usability | Visual | Engineering | Muse Wonder | Gemini 4 Argon |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A Minimal | Fix in place; non-tappable dot on the title capsule | 37 | 39 | 45 | 44 | 42 |
| B Restructure | Dock the pending card above the composer | 29 | 29 | 28 | 31 | 29 |
| C Convention | Messages/Mail style off-screen jump pill plus state rules | 53 | 53 | 51 | 54 | 57 |

A fails criterion 3 (the dot does not take you to the card, and shows colour only). B meets 3 but costs about 230 pt of a 678 pt strip, duplicates the card, and moves a content card into the floating layer.

## Decision
Option C with one graft from A (suggested by Muse Wonder): the demoted composer control uses the solid neutral fill #33322F, the same fill as the card's Save, instead of a translucent fill. Both external judges chose C; neither rejected it.

Rules:
1. No working indicator while any card awaits approval from the user.
2. Working indicator is the DS-06 spec on every board: 28 pt shipped bot avatar in its colour with a 2 pt rotating ring, plus a pill of three neutral dots. No text. VoiceOver says "<agent> is working". One per working agent.
3. A "Needs approval" pill appears above the composer only while the pending card is off screen. Solid content-layer pill (card fill #252523, 1px #2E2D2A border, 36 pt tall, 44 pt hit area, 8 pt warning dot, "Needs approval" 13 pt/600, chevron toward the card). One tap scrolls to the card. It hides when the card is at least half visible. It adds no glass group.
4. While any approval is pending, the card's Send is the only tinted action. The composer trailing control (Send with text, Stop when another agent runs) uses the solid neutral fill #33322F with an ink glyph. This is decided by thread state, not scroll position. With nothing pending, Send (typed) or Stop (running) is the one tinted action.
5. Stop keeps the trailing slot during a run; typed text stays as a draft and Send returns when the run ends. Stopping shows a spinner in the slot, disabled up to 3 s; on failure the slot returns to stop.fill and the working indicator stays (no new copy).
6. The agent sentence "Here is a draft. I have not sent it." is removed when a card follows.

## Why this is the best
- Against A: the cue is tappable and disappears when not needed, and it carries words, not colour alone.
- Against B: it keeps the card in the scroll as DS-06 specifies, keeps the strip's height, and needs no queue for several pending cards (the pill reads "Needs approval" for the nearest one).
- Norms: one tinted action (README), glass only on floating nav (pill is solid), minimal copy (reuses an existing word pair), native SwiftUI (`onScrollVisibilityChange`, `ScrollViewReader`).
- Costs: one new component (pill) and four composer states to draw. With several pending cards the pill points to the nearest; this is not drawn.

## Rebuttal to external judges
Neither external judge disagreed. Muse's graft is adopted. Argon gave no graft.

DS-06-Chat first (spec text and states), then PG-A-Rules and README, then the thread boards.
