# J5: Finance period control: drive the figures or remove it

## Problem
The 30 days / 90 days / 12 months control on PG-B-Finance (and Desk) lights 1, 3 or 12 bars; Runway, Cash and the bars' heights never change (V-pages-R2-1). Runway and Cash are point-in-time, so no range could change them honestly. The hidden-amounts frame leaves Runway as "[N] mo" and keeps bar heights, so masked figures can be inferred (V-pages-R2-5). Unselected bars (`#3A3935` on `#252523`) are about 1.5:1 and there is no baseline (V-pages-R2-6). On Desk the eye sits in the chat half, away from the figures it masks, and the chevron is ambiguous (V-pages-R2-17, STORY-085-R2-6). Nothing says Hide amounts persists (STORY-085-R2-5).
Acceptance: the control either changes numbers and chart or is gone; every figure names its period; the hidden frame states what it masks and masks all inferable values; bars readable at 3:1; eye near the figures it masks; persistence stated.

## Options considered
Weighted scores, max 60 (criteria 1 x3, 2 to 5 x2 each, 6 x1). Lens totals shown as U / V / E.
| Option | Idea | U / V / E | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Minimal | Remove control; label tiles and chart with their period; mask Runway and flatten bars when hidden | 52 / 55 / 58 | 165 |
| B Restructure | Range menu pill in burn card; three data sets (daily, weekly, monthly) | 41 / 44 / 38 | 123 |
| C Convention | Apple Stocks: segmented range under chart, headline average follows | 53 / 52 / 40 | 145 |
External judges (Muse Wonder, Gemini 4 Argon): both pick A. Both score B and C at 3 on criteria 1 (new data sets are invented, hidden-frame decision not addressed) and 2 to 3 on effort.

## Decision
Option A. Remove the range control entirely and make the period part of each label.
Grafts: from C, the chart callout names its month ("Oct [AMOUNT]") so a selected bar is readable. Rejected graft (Muse): a 12-month average headline; it adds a figure that nothing asks for.

## Why this is the best
- Against B: three invented data sets, a hidden menu state, a 30-bar daily chart on a 466pt strip, and no answer to the hidden-amounts question.
- Against C: the range picker still sits above or beside Runway and Cash in a 399pt Duo top half and keeps inviting the false expectation; costs the same three data sets. Usability is a near tie because a shorter window can be asked of the Finance agent in the chat half, which already answers "Why did spend jump last month?".
- Norms: minimal copy (labels shorten, one control removed), progressive disclosure, one source of truth. Costs: no in-page 30/90-day view; revisit when users ask for it with real data.

## Implementation plan
Boards only; see edit list. SwiftUI later: Swift Charts BarMark with 12 months, `chartXSelection`, tile labels as plain Text, hide flag in `@AppStorage`, masked state flattens the series.

## Test plan
Visual checks of every PG-B-Finance frame and Desk at 626x890: no tablist; labels "Runway now", "Cash today", "Monthly burn, 12 months"; hidden frame has "•• mo", "Oct ••••", equal bars; eye top-right of the top half; unselected bar contrast at least 3:1 (`#7E7C75` on `#252523` is about 3.6:1).

## External judge notes
Both voted A, so no rebuttal of the winner is needed. Two grafts were declined: Muse's 12-month average headline (adds an unrequested figure) and Gemini's keeping bare "Runway" and "Cash" labels (V-pages-R2-1 names unlabeled periods as the defect; "now" and "today" add one word each).
