# J5: Scope of new Duo inner, half-folded and laptop boards

## Problem
Most pages describe their inner (1/3 + 2/3), half-folded and laptop poses only in rule-card text. Drawn today: Split (inner Home sidebar + Inbox), Inner (half-folded Inbox + Inbox agent), Desk (laptop, Finance page + thread). Issues: F-duo-001, STORY-085-R1-2/3, F-first-run-home-011/023, STORY-064-R1-1/6, F-duo-014, F-inbox-computer-014/025/026, F-finance-settings-007, STORY-076-R1-7.

Evidence seen: Inner (agent thread beside the list, no message pane), Desk (page top, agent thread bottom, while the Finance rule card says tabs, accounts and Ask go on the bottom half), PG-B-Finance, PG-B-Legal, PG-B-DataRoom (plain lists that Split-style sidebar + detail already explains), PG-A-InboxMessage (Archive and Reply on the outer strip only), PG-A-Computer (key bar and Release on the outer display only).

Acceptance (derived): (1) the two poses with real interaction are drawn: open message on the inner display, Computer in laptop pose with the keyboard open; (2) Desk and its rule card agree; (3) each undrawn page names the template it follows; (4) no new board repeats a decision made elsewhere.

## Options considered
Per-lens weighted totals (max 60; criteria weights 3,2,2,2,2,1), usability / visual / engineering lens:

| Option | Idea | Weighted score (U / V / E) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A Minimal | no new boards, text fixes only | 35 / 33 / 44 |
| B Restructure | five new boards (Finance inner, Legal inner, Message, Data room laptop, Computer) | 47 / 47 / 42 |
| C Templates (Apple Mail / Settings split view) | two new boards on the two interactive poses, three named templates, coverage table | 52 / 52 / 50 |

External judges (same rubric, weighted): Muse Wonder A 32, B 25, C 55; Gemini 4 Argon A 35, B 36, C 57. Both picked C, no graft. Raw scores are in `proposals/J5/external-judges.json`.

## Decision
Winner: C. New real boards now: Inbox message (half-folded) and Computer laptop with keyboard. Rule cards, by design: Finance inner, Legal inner, Cap table, Data room laptop, Sign in inner. Grafts: from A, the explicit coverage line per page; from B, nothing (each B board duplicates Split or Desk).

Why Finance and Legal inner stay text: Split already draws the inner sidebar; the Finance and Legal detail pane is their outer board reflowed. K2 is still changing Finance data and tabs, so a board now would be wrong within a round.

Why Data room laptop stays text: preview top and list below is the same stacked List + detail template as Desk, and the Data room outer board has no preview to copy.

## Why this is the best
- Against A: it leaves the two interactive poses undrawn (Archive, Reply, Cleanup on the inner display; keyboard-open Computer), so build must invent them. Fails acceptance 1.
- Against B: four of five boards copy Split or Desk and have to track K2, K6, K8 and K9 edits. Its message board would also be redundant with a half-folded board.
- Norms: one source per decision, Release in the same slot as Take control (K9), at most three glass groups plus one prominent, nothing interactive at the fold.
- Cost: two boards, one chip, six rule edits. Rules out: a drawn Finance or Legal inner until K2 lands.
