# Visual findings: thread boards, round 1

Boards: PG-A-Thread, PG-A-ThreadStates, PG-B-Consent, PG-A-GroupThread, Inner, DS-06-Chat. Screenshots are in `shots/` (scratch, not committed). Audit output is in `audit.json`. Second opinions are in `second-opinion.json` (muse-wonder and gemini-argon both ran). Tags: [own] my own observation, [muse], [argon] second-opinion models; every external claim was checked against a screenshot first.

Audit notes: no board has page scroll or overflow at its own size. PG-B-Consent is a 956 px two-panel board, so its audit ran at too narrow a viewport and its overflow numbers are an artifact; the full screenshot is correct. The "wrappedControls" flags are false positives (no text wraps in the screenshots). Measured: spacing values 3, 6, 10, 14 are off the 4/8 scale on every board; "Needs approval" contrast is 3.64:1 and "Deny" is 4.13:1; "Privacy details" is 17 px tall.

## Excellent as is

- Message grouping and attribution in PG-A-GroupThread: sender name above each bubble, avatar bottom-left, shipped avatar shapes and colours.
- Failed-send treatment: red outline on the bubble only (text stays normal), with a "Not sent" word and a Retry action. Offline uses a calm banner plus "Waiting to send".
- Consent sheet hierarchy: one ink Allow, quieter Not now, plain-language data sentence.
- Mail approval card: states "I have not sent it", shows To and the body, one prominent Send, and the status is a word (not colour alone).
- Choice card and Ask card in DS-06: stacked 44 pt answers, one prominent Continue only where needed, resolved state collapses to one line.
- Inner two-pane layout: list and thread fit one screen with no scroll, and the selected row ties the thread to the mail.
