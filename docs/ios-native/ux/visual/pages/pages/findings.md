## Second opinion claims rejected after checking the screenshot

- muse: Finance agent avatar is a warning triangle that reads as an alert. It is the shipped avatar shape (orange, with eyes); the real error icon is a separate red triangle.
- muse: Share produces the Saved to Photos toast. The toast follows the photo icon, not share.
- argon: Board materials row lacks its [N] items subtitle in the Data room default frame. It has it.
- argon: Holder detail subtitle is misaligned with the title. Both start at the same x.
- argon: Delete alert is off-center. It is centered on the content area beside the strip.
- muse P0 (Legal scan icon), muse P0 (Add contact), argon P0 (Computer X) downgraded to P2: the actions exist elsewhere on the same board or follow from a deliberate state change.

## Excellent as is

- PG-A-AgentPage chart: line now falls with dated axis and matches the summary sentence (fixes R1 P0).
- PG-A-Computer: eight states, status always in words plus dot color, errors give a cause and one next step (Control ended, Control denied, Reconnecting).
- PG-A-Image delete confirmation: states consequence and that it cannot be undone; Delete is the destructive, non-bold choice.
- PG-B-Legal and PG-B-CapTable failure frames: previous results stay visible, and the retry sits beside the message.
- PG-B-GroupSettings: Delete group is separated at the bottom and its alert names who is affected and that agents are kept.
- PG-B-Finance: one-line amounts hidden state, masked tooltip and rows, Upcoming omitted when empty.
- PG-B-DataRoom uploading row: progress, percent and cancel in one row.
- Rows are 50 pt or taller on every board; strip controls are 44 pt circles.
