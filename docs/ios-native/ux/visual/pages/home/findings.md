# Visual findings: Home and creation boards, round 1

Boards: PG-A-Home, PG-A-HomeNoModel, PG-B-Spaces, Main, PG-B-NewBot, PG-B-NewGroup, PG-B-NewSpace. Captures in `shots/` (scratch), audits in `audit.json`, outside opinions in `second-opinion.json` (both models ran). Found by: [own] = my review, [muse] = muse-wonder, [argon] = gemini-4-argon. "Verified" means I checked the claim against the screenshot or board source.

Audit notes: no page scroll or overflow on any board; no low-contrast text flagged; the audit's small-target hits (inputs 21 px high) are the bare input inside a 44 pt row, so not a defect. Off-scale spacing (6, 10, 14) appears on every board; it is one systemic P3, not per-board (V-home-R1-22).

Rejected outside claims: muse "side strip has no labels, replace with labelled bar" (the strip is the adopted design-system pattern for the outer display; not a finding beyond V-home-R1-15). muse "low contrast secondary text": the audit found no contrast failures. argon "low-contrast typed input in NewSpace": the text is a placeholder, handled as V-home-R1-18.

## Excellent as is

- PG-A-Home: Inbox card and the Activity group keep status in words plus a dot; avatars use the shipped bot shapes and colors; one prominent "+".
- PG-A-HomeNoModel: the empty state is a single sentence with one button, no extra chrome; the sheet's dim-and-close pattern is clear.
- PG-B-Spaces: the space menu is compact with a check on the current space and "New space" and settings separated.
- PG-B-NewGroup: member rows reuse the same avatars and have 51 pt height, comfortably above 44 pt.
- All boards: no overflow or page scroll, 44 pt strip buttons, concentric sheet corners.
