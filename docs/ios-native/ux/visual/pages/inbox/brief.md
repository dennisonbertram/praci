# Brief: Praci iOS inbox boards (round 1)

Praci is a startup operating system. The planned native SwiftUI iPhone app has an inbox for email plus AI agents. These boards are static design mockups for the iPhone Duo outer display (466x678, a thin side toolbar on the left holds floating glass buttons) and inner display (890x626).

Persona: a startup founder who triages email in short sessions on a phone, replies briefly, and wants to clear newsletter clutter in bulk.

Screens and the journey step each serves:
- Split.png: inner display, sidebar plus Inbox list. Step: see what needs a reply, open a message.
- Sheet.png: outer display Inbox list with a "Needs reply" section and "Other mail". Step: triage, open a message, search, filter, compose.
- PG-A-InboxMessage.png: reading one message. Step: read, archive, reply, open an attachment.
- PG-A-Compose.png: three frames, New message, Reply, Reply with title menu (Reply, Reply all, Forward). Step: write and send.
- PG-A-Cleanup.png: four frames, Inbox More menu with Cleanup, senders ticked with Archive 3 and a menu, Done with Undo. Step: bulk-clear noisy senders.

Judge each screen by whether the founder knows where they are, what to do next, and can do it in the fewest taps. Rules: glass only on floating controls, solid content, one prominent action per view, 44 pt targets.
