# Brief

Praci is a planned native SwiftUI iPhone app (Liquid Glass style) for a startup founder who runs a company with AI bots ("agents"). These are design boards on a standard iPhone (393x852 pt); PH-Computer is two states side by side (810x882).

Rules: glass only on floating nav and controls; content is solid; at most three glass groups per view; exactly one tinted prominent action per view; concentric corners; touch targets 44 pt.

Persona: a busy founder, one hand, checking the company between meetings. Wants to see what needs attention, answer or approve in a tap or two, and talk to a bot.

Screens and journey step:
- PH-Home: landing screen. Pick a space, open Inbox, open a page (Finance, Legal, Cap table, Data room, Artifacts), see agent activity, start a chat with a bot.
- PH-Spaces: Home with the space menu open (switch space, new space, space settings).
- PH-Inbox: triage mail, needs-reply first, swipe to archive, compose.
- PH-Thread: chat with the Inbox agent, which drafted a reply; approve it with Send or keep it with Save.
- PH-Computer: watch the team's shared computer (left) and take over control (right), then release.
