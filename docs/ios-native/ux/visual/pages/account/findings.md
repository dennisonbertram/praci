Boards: PG-B-SignIn, PG-B-SignUp, PG-B-ForgotPassword, PG-B-Server, PG-B-Account, PG-B-Settings, PG-B-Models, PG-B-Voice, PG-B-Integrations. All 466x678 Duo outer display. Audits: audit.json. Second opinions: second-opinion.json (muse = meta/muse-wonder, argon = google/gemini-4-argon). Every external claim below was checked against the screenshot in shots/ or the board source; claims I could not confirm are not listed.

Audit notes: no page scroll, no dead bands, no low contrast. The "small target" hits on inputs (21 pt tall) are false positives: each input sits in a 44 pt row. Off-scale spacing (6/10/14) is the shared board grid, identical on every PG-B board, so not a per-board finding.

## Excellent as is

- Sign in and Sign up: single column, one filled primary, SSO clearly subordinate, 44 pt rows, Sign up mirrors Sign in exactly.
- Settings root: grouped inset lists with icon tiles, values right-aligned, X as the modal root close, Account card first.
- Account: three states drawn in one board, destructive action separated from Sign out by a clear gap, delete alert names what is lost.
- Voice: toggles with their meaning in the subtitle ("On - Free, works offline"); Disconnect in red at the end of its group.
- Consistent nav column across the family: time, then back or close, confirm at the bottom; titles, margins and card radii identical on every board.
