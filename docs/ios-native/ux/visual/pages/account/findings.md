# Visual findings: account boards, round 3

Boards: PG-B-SignIn, SignUp, ForgotPassword, Server, Account, Settings, Models, Voice, Integrations (iPhone Duo outer display, 466x678 side strip). Screenshots in `shots/` (scratch, not committed). Audits in `audit.json`; second opinions in `second-opinion.json`.

Audit notes: `pageOverflowX` 239 on every board is the multi-frame grid being wider than the 1207 px capture window, not a defect of the device frame. The layout audit's small-target list only contains text inputs inside 44+ pt row wrappers (false positives) plus the 33x16 inline Terms link on SignUp. Measured: Forgot password, Use a custom server, Sign in, Sign up are all 44 pt tall. Off-scale spacing values (5, 6, 10, 14, 18, 22) appear on every board, so they are a system-wide near-duplicate scale issue, not board-specific (P3, V-account-R3-22).

## Rejected external claims
- [muse] P0 floating back button: the back and close buttons sit in the left side strip below the clock on every board at one consistent position. That is the Duo outer-display layout, not content overlap.
- [muse] and [argon] sub-44 pt "Forgot password?" and "Use a custom server": measured 44 pt tall.
- [muse] P0 Banking "Allow writes" from chat: the button opens the connector page, it does not enable writes.
- [muse] P0 Models and Integrations "dead space" and [muse] P0 Server http warning: downgraded to P3 above.
- My own suspicion that the dimmed Sign in pill in "First-time SSO setup" was too wide: a crop shows it aligned with the form.

## Excellent as is
- State coverage: loading, error, cooldown, keyboard, and expired states are drawn for nearly every flow (SignIn, ForgotPassword, Models, Voice).
- ForgotPassword rules: one Back, Use a different email returning to the right place, Sign in as the single prominent action after reset.
- Primary actions are lifted above the keyboard in every Typing frame.
- Server sheet: clear states, Retry replacing Save, and the "Switch server?" alert naming what is lost.
- Session ended banner on Sign in keeps the email and states that drafts are kept.
- Settings hub and Voice sounds use native-looking grouped rows and switches with clear section labels.
- Mail connector confirm dialogs name the consequence ("You still approve each one").
- Product name Praci, concentric corners, one tinted prominent action per view hold on all nine boards.
