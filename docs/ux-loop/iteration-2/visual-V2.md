# Visual inspection V2 (Inbox, Finance, Legal, chat approvals, artifacts)

Session `uxl2-V2`, 2026-10-20. Target: mock-data demo stack only (web :5381, signed in as the mock founder). Screenshots are in `/tmp/uxloop2/V2/` (outside the repo). Heuristic IDs (L, D, H, C, I, A) come from `ux-visual/references/visual-heuristics.md`.

Method: for each page and state I captured the screen, ran `layout-audit.js` and `geometry-audit.js`, and interacted (hover, Tab, Escape, dialogs, resize). Viewports: 1440x900 everywhere, 375x812 for Inbox, Finance, Legal and chat, 1920x1080 for the shell (Finance, Chief chat, Inbox).

Limits and exclusions:
- The audit's `lowContrast` list reports ratio 1.2 to 1.4 for nearly every text node. Visually the text is clearly readable, so this is a false positive from the dark theme background lookup. I raise no contrast finding.
- Other walkers share this stack. One Chief user message was stored with a newline after every character and renders as a 1260px-tall one-letter column. That is a data artifact from another session's typing, not a product defect. I excluded it.
- I did not click Allow on the consent banners ("Let Praci sort your mail", "Let Praci read your contracts"), Archive, Skip inbox, Archive all, or Dismiss. Whether those confirm or undo is not observed.
- Real third-party sign-ins were not attempted.

## Excellent as is

- Inbox thread dialog and attachment preview: clear action bar, PDF preview fills the dialog, "Add to Data room" and "Download" are placed where expected. Escape closes the preview, then the thread, and focus returns to the clicked row.
- Inbox list at 375 and 1440: sticky header, inner scrolling, Today and Earlier groups, hover actions appear without moving the row, no horizontal overflow at any viewport.
- Pile tabs and sender-grouped Updates and Newsletters lists.
- Finance overview KPIs and the 375 reflow (2x2 tiles, chart and tables stay readable). The Spend table, vendor page and recurring page are clean and consistent.
- Legal Deadlines: overdue state is shown in words and color ("Overdue by 4 days"), with per-row Done and Dismiss.
- Approval card: after the answer it collapses to a one-line result, and the agent then states what it did and what it did not do ("it won't email anyone").
- Artifacts grid, the Markdown viewer and the isolated-preview marker.
- Mobile drawer and chat at 375 (message bubbles, composer pinned at the bottom, "Jump to latest" control).
- Zero page-level scroll at 1440x900, 375x812 and 1920x1080 on every page checked.
