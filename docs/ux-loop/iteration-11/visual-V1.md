# ux-visual V1, iteration 11

Group V1: sign-in, sign-up, first run (Chief card and tour), app shell and sidebar, Settings, Integrations, bot settings panel.
Target: mock-data demo stack (web 5381). Screenshots: `/tmp/uxloop11/V1/` (outside the repo).
Method: layout-audit.js and geometry-audit.js at every page and viewport, tab-order walk, axe, light theme, and touch targets measured with `pointer: coarse` emulation (CDP `Emulation.setTouchEmulationEnabled`) at 375x812.
Sign-up users were invented (`walker-N@northwind.example.test`, generated passwords never written down). The demo account was signed in once to see a multi-space sidebar and the Connected integrations grid; the password was passed from a shell variable and never printed.

## Excellent as is

- Sign-in and sign-up: one clear primary button, labels above fields, show-password control, "Invalid email or password" in red with the fields keeping their values. No layout shift, no scroll, nothing under 44px on a phone.
- The app shell fits one screen at 1280x800, 1440x900 and 1920x1080 with no page scroll; the chat column stays centred and the composer stays pinned at every size.
- The sidebar: Pages menu (show/hide checklist), Create menu (New bot, New group), a bot actions menu with Delete last and in red, unread counts, and collapsed "Page agents" and "Email chats" groups on a multi-space account.
- Phone drawer and shell: every button measured 44x44 or larger with touch emulation; Search, Create and Close are in reach.
- Escape closes Settings and returns focus to the account button.
- Settings on a phone becomes a sheet with a section select, which is the right pattern. Privacy and approvals is short and plain, defaults on the two confirmation switches, and the Advanced disclosure works.
- Light theme: Integrations and the sidebar read cleanly with the same hierarchy.
- Integrations: connected cards with a green "Connected" chip, an Open action and "Used by" avatars are easy to scan; the custom-connection dialog is small and focuses its field.
- Bot settings on a phone is a full-screen panel with a fixed header and the same content as desktop.
