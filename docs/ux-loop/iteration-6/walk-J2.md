## What was excellent

- Creating a bot is two clicks; the name field is focused and selected, the bot greets and asks what it should handle.
- Audience (Internal/External) explains itself only after External is chosen, shows an "External" badge in the header, and exposes the "Can message" list then. Good progressive disclosure.
- Routine "When to run" defaults to Every week, Monday, 9:00 AM with the time zone named.
- Legal Deadlines: sorted by date, overdue in red with "Overdue by 5 days", Done shows an inline Undo (8 s), KPI cards update.
- Data room search shows the matching snippet with highlights; failed file shows the reason and Try again with a toast.
- Cap table agent answered a $2M seed on $9M pre with the SAFEs at the $8M cap: 31.4%. I recomputed it (437,500 SAFE shares, 12.76M post shares) and it is right, and the answer states its assumptions.
- Legal "Last scan failed: The AI key was rejected. [Replace key]" is clear and actionable.
