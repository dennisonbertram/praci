# Visual inspection V1 (iteration 10)

Group V1: sign-in, sign-up, onboarding, first-run tour, app shell and sidebar, Settings, Integrations, bot settings panel.
Target: mock-data demo stack only (web 5381). Screenshots are in `/tmp/uxloop10/V1/` (outside the repo).

## Method notes

- Desktop: `agent-browser` at 1440x900, 1920x1080 (shell) and 1280x800 (Settings fit, bot panel open). Dark theme, plus a light pass on the bot settings panel.
- 375x812 was run with Playwright (`hasTouch`, `isMobile`, `pointer: coarse` confirmed true), because `agent-browser set device` does not turn on a coarse pointer. All touch-target numbers come from that mode. 375 widths without touch were also checked with agent-browser (Integrations).
- `layout-audit.js` and `geometry-audit.js` were run on the shell, Settings and Integrations at 1440x900. Both audits report ratios of about 1.3 for nav labels and "deadSpace" on full-screen overlays. These are false positives (transparent backgrounds, overlay wrappers) and are not reported. I also wrote my own contrast probe; it hit the same colour-parsing problem, so no contrast finding is made. Visually, muted text is readable in dark and light.
- The demo user is shared with other inspector sessions, so chat text, badge counts and one bot setting (Audience of Investor Relations (mock) flipped from External to Internal while I was looking at it) changed during the walk. Anything that depends on that is marked.
- Fresh user for sign-up, onboarding and the tour: `walker-v1l10@northwind.example.test` (invented; password generated, not recorded).
- BLOCKED: Gmail, Google Calendar and every other Connect button need real OAuth; not attempted. The "model" onboarding step does not exist on this stack (system model, no OpenRouter key), so the onboarding walk is the "What do you want me on first?" question and the Connect-your-apps cards.
- Coarse-pointer measurements: every visible control in the phone shell, drawer, account menu and Integrations is at least 44px, except inline text links in chat (`Open Inbox agent`, 21px high) and switches (see V1-09).
