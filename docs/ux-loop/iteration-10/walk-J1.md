## Catalog drift (not an app defect)

- STORY-APP-309/319 describe a "Connect your apps" onboarding page and a tour that opens with the greeting. On the demo the sign-up lands straight in Chief. Chief shows "What do you want me on first?", then Gmail and Google Calendar Connect cards in the thread, and the 9-stop tour starts only after one app is connected (or another page is opened). Catalog steps 1-12 of STORY-APP-319 should be rewritten.
- STORY-APP-209 expects "about 8 months" versus "7.9 mo". The demo data now gives 24.6 months, and Chief and the Finance card agree.

## Praise (observed)

- Finance with no accounts: one clear action ("Add account"), Mercury connects in one click, dashboard appears with consistent numbers across the card, the Runway page and the Finance agent (24.6 mo, Nov 10, 2028).
- Sorted Inbox: To do card with due pills, one-line summaries instead of previews, Newsletters grouped by sender, Digest cards.
- Hide amounts masks money everywhere including the approval toast, and leaves runway months readable.
- Email agent panel: Draft a reply produced a Mail card with From/To, Send and Discard; "Reply with draft" appears only after a card.
- Account menu is short and clear (Settings, Integrations, Hide amounts, Take the tour, Log out).
