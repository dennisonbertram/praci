- Legal on phone: each contract becomes a labelled key-value card with the status at the top right; no horizontal scroll at 375 (scrollWidth equals clientWidth on every page checked).
- Legal graph (once loaded) and the Finance agent chat: pinned Cash and Runway tiles, an editable month-end checklist, and a resolved approval card that reads "Answered: Approve".
- Agent question card: two stacked, full-width answer buttons; once answered the card shows the choice.
- Artifacts: list/grid toggle, master-detail viewer with Download and expand; the numbers in the Cash Dashboard artifact match Finance.
- Layout audit: no horizontal overflow at any viewport; inner scrollers used for the chat list, thread and pages; sticky Inbox header.
