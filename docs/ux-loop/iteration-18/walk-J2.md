## Praise (excellent as is)

- Payment approval card: "Send $1,200.00 to Acme Servo Supply (Mock) from MOCK Checking ..1111?" with the amount on the button, a Details fold and Deny. After approval: "Approved here. Waiting for Mercury", then a plain explanation that the Mercury app must also approve. About 25 s end to end.
- The Allow changes dialog now says "move money and change your Mercury accounts. It asks before each change."
- Integrations page: grouped Connected and Popular, clear Open actions, no clutter.
- Finance page: dense but orderly, no truncation, no overflow (geometry audit clean).
- New bot flow: one name field, More settings on demand, skill form with Name / What it does / Instructions, routine with "Next run Fri, Oct 23, 9:00 AM" shown after Save.
- Data room: consent card disappears silently after Allow; failed view reached from the red badge works.
