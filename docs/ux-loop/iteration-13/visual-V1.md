# Visual inspection V1 (iteration 13)

Scope: sign-in, sign-up, forgot password, first run (Chief welcome, first-question card, tour), app shell and sidebar, Settings, Integrations, bot settings panel.
Target: mock-data demo stack only (web 5381). Viewports 1440x900, 375x812, 1920x1080 (shell), plus 720x450 (200% zoom check). Screenshots: `/tmp/uxloop13/V1/`.
Users: the mock founder signed in (credentials read from the local file into the form, never printed). A fresh invented user (walker-v1-NNN@northwind.example.test, random password) was signed up for first run, tour and new-bot checks. Other walkers share the mock founder, so its sidebar changed during the walk (new chats, a toast); that is not reported.

Method notes
- `layout-audit.js` and `geometry-audit.js` were run on the shell, Integrations, first run and sign-in. The audit's "lowContrast" entries (1.2 to 1.3) are false positives (it cannot resolve the dark background) and were ignored. The sidebar Search placeholder was re-measured from computed colors: about 4.6:1, which passes (iteration 12 reported 3.0:1; that is fixed).
- Touch emulation now works. Attaching a small CDP script that calls `Emulation.setTouchEmulationEnabled` makes `matchMedia("(pointer: coarse)")` true. With it on, targets were measured at 375x812 on the drawer, Integrations, chat, bot panel and Settings. Everything is at least 44x44 except the 32x18 switch graphics, whose rows are 44 px tall (`pointer-coarse:min-h-11`), and the Integrations card titles, which are stretched links covering the whole card. No touch-target finding is filed.
- BLOCKED / not reachable: the onboarding "Connect a model" step (the demo has a default model, so a fresh user goes straight to Chief); Gmail and Google Calendar "Connect" (real OAuth, not attempted). The "Connect your apps" step shows as two cards inside the Chief thread.
- Not exercised: Pub/Sub and any real provider; payment and approval flows (owned by other walkers).

## Excellent as is

- Welcome, sign-in, sign-up and reset pages: one primary action, no scroll at 375 px, errors in words and color, inputs keep their value, red helper turns on invalid input.
- The shell fits one screen at 1440x900 and 1920x1080 with no page scroll; sidebar, chat and Integrations list scroll inside their own panels. At 200% zoom (720x450) it falls back to the phone layout with nothing lost.
- Phone: the drawer, rows (46 px), one "..." per message, composer pinned at the bottom, toast above the composer, and every measured target at least 44 px under touch emulation.
- First-run: question card collapses to the chosen answer, then the connect cards follow in the thread; tour has nine stops with correct rings, "Skip tour" always there, and on a phone each card sits beside its target.
- Command palette: grouped pages with keyboard hints, Escape closes.
- Settings: fixed-size dialog, native select nav on phone, Memory indented under Advanced (iteration 12 V1-20 fixed), Voice folded into Models, "Delete account" is distinct but not dominant.
- Bot panel: the External choice reveals its consequence in one sentence ("Talks to people outside your company...") and adds an "External" badge in the chat header; the model picker reads "System model (OpenAI: GPT-6 Luna)".
- Integrations: 28 px gutters on both sides (iteration 12 V1-11 fixed), connected apps first with "Open Inbox / Open Finance" actions and "Used by" bots, whole-card stretched links.
- Loading state at app boot ("Opening Praci..." then "Still opening... Try again") is honest and recoverable.
