## Excellent
- Sign-up is four fields and one button, ends on Chief in about a second on a warm server, with no model or setup step for a new user.
- Chief's answer to "What should I do first this week?" on an empty account is honest and useful (connect Gmail and Calendar first, no invented tasks).
- Tour: nine stops, clear one-line copy, ring on the target row, Back/Skip on every stop, ends with Ask Chief and Done.
- Inbox: Needs reply group, To do card with due dates, unread dots, badge falls from 9 to 8 after opening one mail; the message dialog has Reply, Archive, Ask agent, and the Email agent panel docks beside the email and returns after sending.
- Finance: Cash, Burn and Runway answer "how long" at a glance (24.2 mo, until October 2028); the Runway page adds a projected-cash chart, run-out date, Count crypto switch and a cash breakdown. Hide amounts masks money in cards, chart axes and chat while keeping runway months readable.
- Legal Deadlines shows "Due in N days" next to each item with Ask agent, Done, Dismiss.
