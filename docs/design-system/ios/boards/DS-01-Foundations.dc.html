3. Layers
Three planes drawn as separate placed boxes, offset so the stacking order reads. Z-order from bottom: content, floating glass, scrim and sheet.
Layer 0 : app background #1C1C1A
Layer 1 : floating glass
back
actions
tab bar
Layer 2 : scrim + sheet (overlay token)
Sheet : content fill, more opaque at full height
Content layer (bottom)
App background, lists, cards, message bubbles, mail approval card. Opaque fills: background, card, muted, chatUser. Scrolls under the glass.
Floating glass layer (middle)
Back/close, action groups, More, tab bar, sidebar. Regular glass, grouped into capsules (3 groups max), one prominent action per view. Never stacked on other glass.
Modal layer (top)
Scrim (overlay token) then a sheet: radius 34, grabber, content fills. Becomes more opaque as it grows to full height.
Rule
Content never gets glass. Glass never gets content (no lists inside a toolbar capsule). The exception is transient control knobs such as a switch thumb while dragging.

6. Iconography
Inline stroke SVG that stands for an SF Symbol. In production on iOS use the SF Symbol itself; these drawings only reproduce its meaning in prototypes. Pick the SF Symbol weight that matches 17pt text (regular or medium).
Stroke style. 24 x 24 viewBox, stroke-width 2 (1.8 for dense bars), round caps and joins, no fill except dots (ellipsis) and stop.fill. Drawn at 24pt inside a 44 x 44pt hit target, centred. Colour is currentColor: foreground on glass, onPrimary on prominent, mutedForeground for passive. Icon-only buttons need an aria-label.
Pairing. Fill the symbol when selected (star to star.fill, bell to bell.fill). Use the .slash variants for off or muted, never a color change alone. Do not mix outlined and filled icons in one group.

8. Motion
Short notes. These describe behavior to build with system components; timing numbers marked est. are ours.
Glass morph
Controls inside one GlassEffectContainer merge and split as items appear or disappear (for example the action group growing a Send button). Use a spring, response about 0.35s, damping 0.8 (est.: Apple publishes no durations). Do not morph across groups that stay separate.
Tab bar minimize
On scroll down the floating tab bar collapses to the selected tab; scroll up or tap restores it. Content scrolls under it with the scroll edge effect fading text behind the glass. Apple names the behavior; the distance threshold is not published.
Sheet opacity
A half sheet floats inset from the edges with a grabber and a translucent glass background. As it is dragged toward full height the background becomes more opaque and the corners straighten against the screen. Keep text on the content fill, not on the translucent part.
Reduce Motion / Transparency
With Reduce Motion, replace morphs with a fade of 0.15s. With Reduce Transparency, glass renders as a solid card fill with the same border; all layouts must still read.
