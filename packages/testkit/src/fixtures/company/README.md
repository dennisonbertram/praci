# Company fixtures

All names, identities, domains, sources and company history are invented. Reserved `.example` domains are never fetched.

`ground-truth.ts` is the independently authored oracle for a 2021–2024 company history. It defines true entities, employment periods, relationships and dated events with source IDs. It is not generated by an extractor or copied from the result being evaluated. `corpus.ts` materializes source text from that authored world plus noise, a contradictory draft, and three planted instructions. The deterministic content hashes support replay tests.

The Northwind corpus has 200 mail threads, 40 documents, 600 messages in 12 public channels and 150 tracker items. Its 30 people include two Sams and a person with three work emails who leaves and returns. It includes two funding rounds, five customers, three vendors, a law firm, two legal matters, three products and a year-only event. Seabright is a second invented company with a colliding work identity, for storage in a separate owner or space.

Source IDs are fixture-local identifiers, not database primary keys. Import tests must allocate their own spaces, users and entities, map oracle IDs to returned entity IDs, and persist evidence through the scoped Company writer. `statement` is the expected supporting text (at most 240 characters); the Series A draft is explicitly contradictory, not another true event. Source items tagged `instruction_like` must never confer authority to confirm facts or execute tools.

This fixture proves plumbing, regression behavior and isolation when used with recorded output. It makes no claim about live extraction quality. The later evaluation stage owns the extractor recording, independent comparison and negative-mutation gates.
